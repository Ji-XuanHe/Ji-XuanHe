### About Me

I am an incoming Ph.D. student (direct admission) at the **School of Computer Science and Technology, Xi'an Jiaotong University**, starting in 2026. I received my B.Eng. from **Hefei University of Technology** (2022 – 2026).

My research lies in **low-level vision** and **computational imaging**, with a focus on bridging classical imaging physics with modern deep learning.

### Research Interests

- **Low-Light Image Super-Resolution** — physics-informed degradation modeling for real-world SR
- **Hyperspectral Image Reconstruction** — plug-and-play spectral refinement
- **Learning with Limited Paired Data** — semi-/un-supervised strategies for image restoration
- **Vision–Language Agents** — multimodal foundation models for perception and reasoning

### Recent Work

- **DTP** — A degradation-aware framework for low-light image super-resolution. *Accepted at ICME 2026.*  
  [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27301)] [[Code](https://github.com/JXVision/DTP)]
- **SR²-Net** — A plug-and-play spectral refinement module for hyperspectral image reconstruction. *Under review at IJCAI 2026.*  
  [[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21338)]
- **[ccg-paper](https://github.com/Ji-XuanHe/ccg-paper)** — An open-source multi-model collaborative workflow (Claude Code · Codex · Gemini) for top-tier conference paper writing, reviewing, and rebuttal.
